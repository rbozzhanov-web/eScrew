⚠️ Before you start

Important: You must first log in to AIMS at https://aims.airastana.com/ecrew

  1. Open https://aims.airastana.com/ecrew in Safari
  2. Sign in with your crew credentials
  3. Navigate to your Schedule or Dashboard page
  4. Then continue with the bookmark setup below

One-time setup

1
Copy bookmark

Tap the button below

2
Create Safari bookmark

In Safari: Tap Share → Add Bookmark → Name it "eScrew AIMS" → Edit URL and paste

Each time you use it

  1. In eScrew: Tap the A button (opens AIMS)
  2. In AIMS: Sign in and navigate to your schedule
  3. In AIMS: Tap the "eScrew AIMS" bookmark
  4. Wait while eScrew reads the roster from your authenticated AIMS session
  5. When "✓ Roster ready" appears, tap "Send to eScrew"
  6. Back in eScrew: Roster appears ✓
