eScrew AIMS Capture

One-time Safari setup. After installation, Capture starts automatically whenever AIMS opens in Safari.

Install once
  1. Use a Safari userscript extension/manager on iPhone or iPad and enable it for aims.airastana.com.
  2. Open the eScrew Capture userscript below and install/enable it.
  3. That is the only setup step. No Safari bookmarklet is needed afterwards.
Open eScrew Capture userscript
Normal use after setup
  1. In eScrew tap AIMS.
  2. Safari opens AIMS. Sign in if required.
  3. Open My Schedule normally.
  4. Capture runs automatically. When ready, tap Copy roster to eScrew.
  5. Return to eScrew and tap AIMS again to paste/import.

No Capture bookmark, Period change, Zoom change, or manual arming is required during normal use.

Privacy boundary

The connector only keeps a sanitized roster-shaped response for the clipboard handoff. Password entry remains in AIMS/Safari.
